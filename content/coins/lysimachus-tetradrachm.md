A magnificent tetradrachm issued under the Diadoch king Lysimachus, depicting the deified Alexander the Great with the horns of Ammon on the obverse, and Athena enthroned holding a Nike on the reverse. 

Struck at the height of the Thracian Kingdom's power, this piece displays crisp details, brilliant cabinet luster, and exceptional artistic style typical of the Amphipolis mint during the early 3rd century BC.
